From website address to working assistant in three steps

No developer, no training data to prepare, no account to create.

1

Paste your address

Greetwell reads your home page and the pages that matter most: pricing, services, about, FAQ and contact. It writes a profile of your business and the questions worth asking your leads.

2

Try it and tune it

Chat with your assistant in a live preview. Change its name, greeting, colour and qualifying questions, and add notes for anything your site doesn't say.

3

Add one line of code

Copy a single script tag into your site. It works with WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow and plain HTML. Leads start arriving in your dashboard.

Built to get you the right conversations, not more noise

Most chat widgets collect every email address and leave you to sort them out. Greetwell tells you who is ready to buy and why.

Answers from your content only

The assistant answers from what your site and your notes say. When it doesn't know, it says so and offers to pass the question to you. It never invents a price or a promise.

Leads scored, with reasons

Every lead gets a score from 0 to 100 and a hot, warm or cold label, based on intent, timeline, budget and how reachable they are. You see exactly why.

Helpful first, then curious

Visitors get their answer before they are asked for anything. The assistant asks one natural question at a time, the way a good receptionist would.

Every conversation, in full

Read the whole transcript behind each lead, and the chats that didn't become leads, so you learn what visitors ask and where your site falls short.

Leads go where you work

Send each new lead to Slack, Zapier, Make or your CRM through a webhook the moment it is captured, or export everything to CSV.

Speaks your customer's language

The assistant replies in whatever language the visitor writes in, and greets them in the language of your site.

Serverless on AWS, from the first request

Nothing runs when nobody is chatting, so a small business assistant costs cents to operate. The whole stack is one CloudFormation template.

CloudFront + S3Serves this site and the widget worldwide, and fronts the API.
API Gateway + LambdaOne Python function for the API, one background worker that reads sites.
DynamoDBAssistants, conversations, leads and rate-limit counters in a single table.
Amazon BedrockClaude writes the business profile and holds the conversations.
GuardrailsPer-visitor, per-assistant and global daily caps. Safe fetching of user-supplied addresses.

Small sites fit in the prompt whole and are cached, so answers stay grounded without a vector database. Larger sites are narrowed per question with BM25 retrieval in the Lambda itself.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. When your assistant is built you get a private dashboard link. Bookmark it: the link is the key to your dashboard, and this browser remembers it for you too.

What if my site is built with JavaScript or has very little text?

If Greetwell can't read enough from your site, it tells you, and you can describe your business in a text box instead. You can also add notes at any time for things your site doesn't mention.

Will it make things up?

It is instructed to state only what your content says and to admit when it doesn't know, then offer to pass the question to you. Like any AI it can still make mistakes, so the widget tells visitors it is an AI assistant.

What does it cost?

Greetwell is free while it is in beta, with a daily message limit per assistant. Paid plans for higher volume are planned.

What happens to conversation data?

Conversations are deleted automatically after 45 days. Leads stay until you delete them. You can delete your assistant and everything it stored from the dashboard at any time.

How do I install it?

Copy the one-line script tag from your dashboard and paste it before the closing </body> tag of your site, or into your site builder's "custom code" setting.
